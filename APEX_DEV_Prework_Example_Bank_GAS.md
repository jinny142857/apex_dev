# APEX DEV — Categorized pre-work problem bank (GAS edition)

교실과 학교에서 반복되는 실제 문제를 다룬 **28개 예시**다. 참가자 선택 화면에는 **Category → Title → Problem statement**를 표시하고, 선택한 경우 **Problem statement 한 문장만** `Your problem`에 불러온다. 문장은 자유롭게 수정·교체할 수 있다. 아래의 *Possible app direction*은 진행자 참고용이며, 문제정의 입력창이나 참가자의 PRD를 자동으로 채우지 않는다.

각 문장은 **WHO struggles to [SPECIFIC TASK / PAIN] when [SITUATION] because [ROOT CAUSE]**의 구조를 따른다. ‘앱이 없기 때문’처럼 해결책을 원인으로 삼지 않았다. 학교마다 학년, 행정 체계, 언어, 기기·인터넷 환경이 다르므로 참가자가 자기 맥락에 맞게 문장을 바꾸는 것을 권장한다.

## A. Shared spaces, equipment & supplies | 공용 공간·기자재·물품 (5)

**공통 개발 방향:** 여러 교사가 같은 목록에서 예약·대여·요청 상태를 확인하거나 바꾼다.

### A1. Shared room booking
**Problem statement:** Teachers who use a shared room struggle to know whether it is available when planning lessons because booking changes are passed on verbally or written on a schedule that is not updated for everyone.

*Possible app direction:* 날짜·시간별 예약 현황 보기 → 예약 추가·취소 → 공동 목록 갱신.

### A2. Equipment loans
**Problem statement:** Teachers who share projectors, speakers, or lab kits struggle to locate an item and arrange its next use when preparing a lesson because borrowing and return details are not recorded in one place.

*Possible app direction:* 물품별 대여 상태·반납 예정일 기록, 반납 처리.

### A3. Consumable supplies
**Problem statement:** Teachers who rely on shared classroom supplies struggle to request replacements before materials run out when preparing practical activities because remaining stock and pending requests are known only to whoever last handled the supplies.

*Possible app direction:* 재고·요청 등록, 담당자 처리 상태 공유.

### A4. Repair requests
**Problem statement:** Teachers who report broken classroom equipment struggle to know whether a repair has been assigned or completed when the item is needed again because reports and updates are exchanged separately with different staff members.

*Possible app direction:* 고장 신고 → 담당자 배정 → 처리 단계·완료 표시.

### A5. Shared teaching materials
**Problem statement:** Teachers who use a limited set of shared textbooks or activity materials struggle to prepare enough copies for their classes when several classes need the same set because nobody can easily see how many are available or in use.

*Possible app direction:* 교구 세트별 보유·사용 수량과 대여 현황.

## B. School events & routine administration | 행사·일상 행정 (5)

**공통 개발 방향:** 여러 담당자가 한 행사의 업무, 제출, 승인, 변경 이력을 함께 확인한다.

### B1. Event task coordination
**Problem statement:** Staff members organizing a school event struggle to know who is responsible for each remaining task when plans change close to the event because assignments and updates are spread across meetings, messages, and paper notes.

*Possible app direction:* 행사별 할 일·담당자·기한·진행 상태 공동 관리.

### B2. Permission form collection
**Problem statement:** Teachers collecting permission forms for school activities struggle to identify whose forms still need follow-up when several forms are due at different times because returned papers and submission notes are kept separately.

*Possible app direction:* 활동별 제출 여부와 후속 확인 상태. 시연에는 가상 명단 사용.

### B3. Notice distribution
**Problem statement:** Teachers responsible for sending school notices struggle to confirm which classes have received the latest version when details change after the first notice because copies and corrections are distributed through different channels without a shared delivery record.

*Possible app direction:* 공지 버전·반별 전달 확인 및 정정 표시.

### B4. Meeting follow-up
**Problem statement:** Staff members who leave a school meeting with assigned actions struggle to see what remains unfinished before the next meeting because decisions and deadlines are embedded in notes that each attendee keeps differently.

*Possible app direction:* 결정 사항별 담당자·기한·완료 상태 공유.

### B5. Library book circulation
**Problem statement:** Teachers or school staff who lend books from a small school library struggle to tell which titles can be borrowed when students request them because loans and returns are recorded in different notebooks or at different desks.

*Possible app direction:* 도서별 대출·반납 기록과 현재 이용 가능 여부.

## C. Teacher collaboration & continuity | 교사 협업·업무 인계 (4)

**공통 개발 방향:** 교사 간 자료, 진행 상황, 인계 사항을 한곳에 쌓고 찾아본다.

### C1. Reusing lesson activities
**Problem statement:** Teachers teaching the same topic struggle to find an activity a colleague has already tried when preparing the next lesson because plans and notes are stored in personal notebooks or separate files without a shared way to search them.

*Possible app direction:* 주제별 활동 카드 등록·검색·간단한 사용 메모.

### C2. Cover lesson handover
**Problem statement:** Teachers covering a colleague's class struggle to know what students have already learned and what materials they need when taking over at short notice because the current plan and classroom notes are held by the absent teacher.

*Possible app direction:* 반·날짜별 수업 목표, 자료 위치, 진행 상태 인계.

### C3. Curriculum progress across classes
**Problem statement:** Teachers who teach the same subject to different classes struggle to coordinate the next shared activity when classes are moving at different speeds because each class's progress is recorded in a separate planning book.

*Possible app direction:* 반별 단원·진도·다음 활동 공동 보기.

### C4. Handover of recurring duties
**Problem statement:** Staff members taking over a recurring school duty struggle to know which steps and contacts still matter when the responsible person changes because instructions are passed on informally and past decisions are hard to find.

*Possible app direction:* 업무별 절차·담당·마지막 처리 내역 기록.

## D. Classroom activities & participation | 수업 운영·참여 (4)

**공통 개발 방향:** 교사가 활동을 설정하고 참여·진행 상태를 기록한다. 학생 직접 입력은 컴퓨터실이나 공유 기기가 있는 경우에만 선택한다.

### D1. Group task progress
**Problem statement:** Teachers running group activities struggle to see which groups need help before time runs out because progress is reported at different moments while the teacher is moving between groups.

*Possible app direction:* 모둠별 과제 단계·도움 요청 등록, 교사용 현황판.

### D2. Shared-device activity turns
**Problem statement:** Teachers using a limited number of computers struggle to give every group a turn during a practical activity because the order and remaining time are tracked informally while the lesson continues.

*Possible app direction:* 모둠별 사용 순서·차례 완료 기록; 학생 개인 기기 불필요.

### D3. Question queue
**Problem statement:** Teachers leading a busy class struggle to respond to student questions fairly during independent work because several students ask for help at once and the order is easy to lose while teaching.

*Possible app direction:* 교사 또는 공용 기기로 질문 순서 등록·완료 표시.

### D4. Participation over time
**Problem statement:** Teachers who want more students to contribute struggle to notice who has rarely had a chance to speak across several lessons because participation is observed in the moment but not consistently recorded afterward.

*Possible app direction:* 날짜별 참여 표시와 참여 기회 분포 보기; 낙인·공개 순위 피하기.

## E. Assessment, feedback & learning progress | 평가·피드백·학습 경과 (5)

**공통 개발 방향:** 교사가 여러 활동의 기록을 축적해 미제출, 공통 어려움, 후속 지도 대상을 파악한다.

### E1. Assignment collection
**Problem statement:** Teachers collecting work on paper struggle to see which students still need to submit each assignment when several tasks are collected over a week because submission marks are written on separate sheets and are difficult to compare.

*Possible app direction:* 과제별 제출 상태와 확인 날짜 기록.

### E2. Common misconceptions
**Problem statement:** Teachers reviewing classwork struggle to identify which questions need to be revisited with the whole class when many students make similar mistakes because observations are scattered across marked papers and informal notes.

*Possible app direction:* 문항·주제별 어려움 건수 집계와 재지도 메모; 익명 집계로 시작 가능.

### E3. Feedback follow-through
**Problem statement:** Teachers who give written feedback struggle to see whether students have acted on a requested revision when work is resubmitted on different days because feedback and revised work are kept in separate piles.

*Possible app direction:* 과제별 수정 요청·재제출·확인 상태.

### E4. Learning progress over a term
**Problem statement:** Teachers who assess the same learning skill several times struggle to recognize which students still need practice when planning a follow-up lesson because results from different activities are stored in separate class records.

*Possible app direction:* 기술·활동별 간단한 숙달 기록과 후속 활동 메모.

### E5. Exit-ticket patterns
**Problem statement:** Teachers collecting brief reflections at the end of a lesson struggle to decide what to explain again next time because responses arrive on paper or through shared devices and must be sorted before patterns become visible.

*Possible app direction:* 교사의 응답 입력 또는 공유 기기 제출 → 주제별 모아 보기.

## F. Attendance, student support & family contact | 출결·학생 지원·가정 소통 (5)

**공통 개발 방향:** 교사가 필요한 상태와 후속 조치를 기록한다. 이 범주의 시연 데이터는 반드시 가상·익명으로 준비한다.

### F1. Attendance follow-up
**Problem statement:** Teachers who follow up on repeated absences struggle to notice which students need a check-in when reviewing several days of attendance because daily marks are kept on separate pages and patterns are not easy to see.

*Possible app direction:* 날짜별 출결 입력 → 반복 결석 표시 → 교사 확인 여부.

### F2. Catch-up after absence
**Problem statement:** Teachers helping students return after an absence struggle to coordinate what work still needs to be completed when different subject teachers assign make-up tasks because each teacher keeps a separate record of missed work.

*Possible app direction:* 학생별 누락 과제·담당 과목·완료 상태 공동 기록.

### F3. Student support handover
**Problem statement:** Teachers supporting a student over several weeks struggle to continue agreed classroom support when another teacher takes responsibility because actions tried and next steps are recorded in private notes that are not handed over consistently.

*Possible app direction:* 제한된 교사끼리 지원 조치·후속 확인 기록; 실제 학생 정보는 시연 금지.

### F4. Family meeting requests
**Problem statement:** Teachers arranging short meetings with families struggle to find a time that has not already been taken when requests arrive through notes, calls, and in-person conversations because availability and confirmations are tracked separately.

*Possible app direction:* 교사가 요청 입력·시간 확정·일정 확인; 가족 계정 필수 아님.

### F5. Home communication follow-up
**Problem statement:** Teachers who send important messages home struggle to know which families may need another contact attempt when replies come back through different channels because the date, channel, and response status are not recorded together.

*Possible app direction:* 교사가 전달·응답·후속 연락 상태 기록; 가정의 상시 접속 가정 없음.

## Selection guidance for facilitators

- **가장 단순한 공동 데이터 예시:** A1, A2, B1, B4, E1. 목록 보기 → 한 건 추가·변경 → 다시 목록 확인으로 시연 가능하다.
- **교사 중심 예시:** A–C, D3–D4, E, F. 학생 개인 기기나 가족 계정을 전제로 하지 않는다.
- **공유 기기 사용을 살릴 예시:** D1–D2, E5. 학교에 기기가 있는 경우에만 학생 입력을 더한다.
- **실습 범위 조절:** 한 번에 하나의 핵심 흐름부터 만든다. 로그인, 자동 알림, 권한별 화면, 복잡한 승인 단계는 문제마다 필수 요건이 아니다.
- **개인정보:** 출결, 성적, 상담·지원, 가족 연락의 실제 내용은 실습 웹앱에 입력하지 않는다. 가상 명단이나 익명 건수로 작동을 확인한다. 이후 실제 운영 여부는 학교의 개인정보·접근권한 기준에 맞춰 별도로 판단한다.
- **연결 환경:** 여러 사람이 같은 데이터를 쓰는 예시는 접속 가능한 동안 서버와 통신하는 흐름이다. 종이 수합 후 교사가 입력하는 방식으로도 시작할 수 있다. 인터넷 없이 자동 동기화되는 것으로 설명하지 않는다.
