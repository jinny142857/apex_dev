# Pre-work: Define a School Challenge

## Purpose

This workshop is about building a web app — through vibe coding — that helps with a real problem you face as a teacher or in your school's daily work. Before we start, name that problem clearly. A specific, well-defined problem is what makes the app you build during the workshop actually useful.

## What each part means

- **WHO** — the person or group affected. Be specific ("new homeroom teachers," not "teachers").
- **TASK** — the specific thing they are trying to do.
- **SITUATION** — when or where it happens.
- **ROOT CAUSE** — why it keeps happening. A cause, not a solution.

## Before you submit

- Describe one specific, recurring challenge — not a one-off inconvenience.
- State the cause, not an app idea you already have in mind.
- Someone outside your school should be able to understand who has the problem.
- No real student names, photos, or other identifying details.
