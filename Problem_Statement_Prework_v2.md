# Pre-work: Identify an Administrative Challenge at School

## Purpose

Before the workshop, identify one specific, recurring task related to teacher workload or school administration. You will use this challenge to explore how a simple web app might make the work easier.

Focus on the problem before thinking about a solution. Choose a task you have experienced or observed. Do not include real student names or other sensitive personal information.

## Problem Statement Template

> **[WHO] struggle(s) to [SPECIFIC TASK OR PAIN] when [SITUATION / CONTEXT] because [ROOT CAUSE].**

## What Each Part Means

- **WHO:** The person or group experiencing the difficulty. You may describe what they are responsible for or trying to achieve.
- **SPECIFIC TASK OR PAIN:** The particular task that is difficult, repetitive, confusing, or time-consuming.
- **SITUATION / CONTEXT:** When, where, or under what circumstances the difficulty occurs.
- **ROOT CAUSE:** The underlying reason the difficulty occurs. Describe the cause, not a possible solution.

## Examples

### Example 1: Sharing Information About School Events

> **[Teachers involved in planning a school event]** struggle to **[find the latest event information and understand their assigned tasks]** when **[preparing for and running a school event]** because **[updates, task assignments, and related documents are shared across different messages and platforms, with no single place to check the current information].**

**Breakdown**

- **WHO:** Teachers involved in planning a school event
- **SPECIFIC TASK OR PAIN:** Finding the latest information and understanding assigned tasks
- **SITUATION / CONTEXT:** Preparing for and running a school event
- **ROOT CAUSE:** Updates, tasks, and documents are scattered across messages and platforms

### Example 2: Managing Student Consent Forms

> **[Teachers responsible for collecting student consent forms]** struggle to **[track submitted forms and follow up on missing ones]** when **[collecting consent for multiple school activities]** because **[each activity uses a different form and submission records are managed separately on paper or in different files].**

**Breakdown**

- **WHO:** Teachers responsible for collecting student consent forms
- **SPECIFIC TASK OR PAIN:** Tracking submitted forms and following up on missing ones
- **SITUATION / CONTEXT:** Collecting consent for multiple school activities
- **ROOT CAUSE:** Different forms and separate records make the collection status difficult to manage

## Before You Submit

- Choose one specific, recurring task related to teacher workload or school administration.
- Describe when the difficulty occurs.
- Explain why it occurs.
- Focus on the work problem; do not name an app or tool as the problem.
- Do not include real student names, photos, or other sensitive personal information.
